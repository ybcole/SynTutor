# Grammar Coverage Map

Goal: document what SynTutor covers vs what remains, to reach scope-bounded completeness for standard written English.

## Scope (in-scope)
- Standard written English morphosyntax: agreement, tense/aspect, articles, plurals, pronouns, comparatives/superlatives, modals, voice, punctuation (basic), determiners, coordination/subordination, modifier placement, word forms.
- Only unambiguous grammatical errors are flagged as `ungrammatical`; ambiguous/style cases → `suggestion` or `unverified`.

## Out of scope (by design)
- Pragmatics, discourse coherence beyond anaphora
- Style preferences not clearly ungrammatical
- Heavy dialectal/idiolectal variation
- Pure semantics without clear syntactic constraint
