## Source and teaching decisions

The [storyboard](../planning/syntax-foundations-chapter-3-storyboard.md) follows Burton-Roberts, *Analysing Sentences*, third edition, printed pp. 46–66. This is the same copy identified in the Chapter 1 source receipt, with PDF pages 61–81 for this chapter. The source check used the chapter text and visually checked diagrams on printed pp. 51–53, 57, and 61–62.

| Learner task | Printed anchors and source check |
| --- | --- |
| Distinguish a noun from the NP it heads | Pp. 47–53, [7], [14], [18]. The noun “lamps” heads “the paper lamps”. “They” replaces the whole NP. The separate name and pronoun diagrams preserve the V3 distinction between the word and the phrase. |
| Trace a degree word inside an AdvP | Pp. 54–56 and 62, in-text diagrams (a–b). In “quite clearly visible”, “quite” modifies “clearly” within AdvP. “Visible” heads the AP. In “very visible”, “very” modifies the adjective directly. |
| Compare prepositional phrases | Pp. 56–57, [19a–b]. “Beside” and “near” each have their own landmark NP. Locative “there” is a one-word PP in this analysis. |
| Explain coordination | Pp. 57–62, [41b]. Two complete PPs and “and” are sisters under the outer PP. The transfer accepts either quoted spans or an equivalent explanation of both conjuncts and the whole PP. |
| Review assessed outcomes | Opening items cover NP replacement, head and degree, manner, PP span, and coordination. Closing items cover names as NPs, AP, AdvP, locative PP, and coordination. Each closing item has option-specific feedback. |

The early notation is intentional. The examples are original and the tree canvases show phrase fragments in the chapter’s notation. Independent linguistic review has not been recorded. The 9 min 55 sec budget is an estimate pending a first-time learner trial.
