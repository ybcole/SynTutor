# Form 1 — Student Questionnaire

## B. Syntactic Assessment (Form A: Pretest)

Instructions: Choose the single best answer.

1. Which sentence is grammatically correct?
   - A. The boy in the red shirt runs quickly.
   - B. The boy in the red shirt run quickly.
   - C. A boy in the red shirt runs quick.
   - D. The boy at the red shirt runs quickly.

2. Which bracketing shows one noun phrase as the subject?
   Sentence: *The old man from the village smiled.*
   - A. [The old man] [from the village] [smiled]
   - B. [The] [old man from the village] [smiled]
   - C. [The old man from] [the village] [smiled]
   - D. [The old] [man from the village] [smiled]

3. The collection of old books ___ on the shelf.
   - A. is  B. are  C. be  D. were

4. Either Tom or Ann ___ here.
   - A. is  B. are  C. be  D. am

5. Which sentence contains a determiner-stacking violation?
   - A. The my book is new.
   - B. My book is new.
   - C. The new book is mine.
   - D. A new book arrived.

6. Which sentence has a determiner with no head noun in an argument position?
   - A. I hate your.
   - B. I hate your idea.
   - C. I hate ideas.
   - D. I hate your new idea.

7. ___ likes music.
   - A. I  B. Me  C. My  D. Mine

8. Tom called ___.
   - A. her  B. she  C. hers  D. herself

9. Which sentence uses the correct article?
   - A. An hour passed.
   - B. A hour passed.
   - C. An university opened.
   - D. A apple fell.

10. Which sentence is ungrammatical because of a missing article?
   - A. Dog is barking.
   - B. The dog is barking.
   - C. Dogs are barking.
   - D. A dog barks.

11. Which sentence places a modifier after its head?
   - A. The car red is fast.
   - B. The red car is fast.
   - C. The fast red car stopped.
   - D. A fast car passed.

12. He runs ___.
   - A. quickly  B. quick  C. quicker  D. quickness

13. Which sentence violates quantifier–noun agreement?
   - A. Less cookies remain.
   - B. Fewer cookies remain.
   - C. Less water remains.
   - D. Fewer students remain.

14. Which form is a double comparative?
   - A. more smarter  B. smarter  C. more smart  D. smartest

15. Which sentence is grammatically correct?
   - A. She can go.  B. She can goes.  C. She can went.  D. She can going.

16. Which is a fragment lacking an independent clause?
   - A. Because I was late.
   - B. I was late.
   - C. Because I was late, I apologized.
   - D. I apologized because I was late.

17. Which sentence has a capitalization error?
   - A. he runs fast.
   - B. He runs fast.
   - C. He runs fast!
   - D. He runs fast, too.

18. Which sentence is ungrammatical because the VP is headed by a noun?
   - A. I am agree.
   - B. I agree.
   - C. I am in agreement.
   - D. I agree completely.

19. Which sentence uses the correct article?
   - A. An elephant arrived.
   - B. A elephant arrived.
   - C. A hour passed.
   - D. An university opened.

20. Which sentence is ungrammatical because of relative-clause agreement?
   - A. The boys who plays football left.
   - B. The boys who play football left.
   - C. The boy who plays football left.
   - D. The boy who played football left.
