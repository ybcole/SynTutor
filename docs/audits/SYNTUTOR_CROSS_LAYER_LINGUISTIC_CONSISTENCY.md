## 1. End-to-End Root Cause Analysis (`"I am scares tomorrow."` & General Failure Classes)

Tracing `"I am scares tomorrow."` and 12 neighboring grammatical/ungrammatical controls through `spaCy → Benepar → FINAL-D1 projection → ruleset/matrix → findings → learner_explanations → explanation-evidence.js` identified six systemic cross-layer defects rather than isolated single-sentence bugs:

1. **Unlicensed Empty Preposition (`P -> E`) on Bare Temporal NP Adjuncts ([server/final_d1_projection.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/final_d1_projection.py)):**
   - `adjunct()` (line 902) and nominal postmodification (line 840) routed *all* bare temporal NPs (`tomorrow`, `yesterday`, `today`, `last night`, `next week`) through `temporal_pp()`, unconditionally fabricating `PP -> P(E [EMPTY]) + NP -> DET[EMPTY] + NOM -> N`.
   - Direct inspection of Burton–Roberts textbook IMG reference records (`ch5-d3` *"She hardly slept last night"*, `ch6-69` *"They were going to India next week last week"*) confirmed that bare temporal NPs attach directly as `VP -> [VP, NP]` adjuncts; **zero** textbook IMG records contain `P -> E`.
2. **Cross-Layer Lexical Category vs. Dependency Function Contradiction ([server/learner_explanations.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py)):**
   - On `"I am scares tomorrow."`, spaCy internally assigned `pos_='ADJ', tag_='NNS', dep_='acomp'` while Benepar projected `(NP (NNS scares))`.
   - `_word_morphology_summary()` and `_runtime_description()` checked only `type == ftag` (missing `fpos` vs. `type`/`ftag` contradictions) and blindly mapped `fdep='acomp'` onto `N` nodes, emitting *"“scares” is a plural noun. It functions and serves as the predicative adjective complement of “am”."* (also containing a prose concatenation defect *"It functions and serves as"*). Even grammatical plural copular nouns (*"Those are scares."*) received `"predicative adjective complement"` when spaCy tagged `acomp`.
3. **Unlicensed / Generic Empty-Constituent Explanations ([server/learner_explanations.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py)):**
   - Unfilled Burton–Roberts zero-determiner slots (`DET [EMPTY]` before bare plural/uncountable/temporal nouns like `tomorrow` or `scares`) and structural empty placeholders received generic *"omitted part of the sentence"* or *"omitted preposition"* prose without identifying the syntactic licensor (or qualifying tentative/conflicted empty templates).
4. **Missing Copular Predicative Complement & Auxiliary `be + VBZ` Detection/Propagation ([server/ruleset.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/ruleset.py), [server/findings.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/findings.py)):**
   - Strictly singular personal pronoun subjects (`I`, `he`, `she`) + copular `be` (`am`, `is`, `was`) + bare plural/inflected `-s` complements (*"I am scares tomorrow."*, *"I am teachers."*, *"He is doctors."*) escaped rule detection, yielding `verdict: unverified` and `findings: []`.
   - Auxiliary `be` + finite 3sg `-s` verb (*"She is sleeps today."*) fired `C-AUX-FORM` in `matrix.py`, but was dropped in `findings.py` because spaCy tagged `sleeps` as `NOUN/attr` rather than `aux` dependent of `is`.
   - `_evidence_token_ids()` in `findings.py` inspected only `evidence['supporting']`, missing `evidence['conflict']` populated by `_tree_evidence()` in `ruleset.py`, so related non-erroneous participant tokens (`I`, `am`) were not surfaced in `related_token_ids`.
   - `C-INTRANSITIVE-OBJECT` falsely flagged coordinated temporal `NP + PP` adjuncts (*"She will arrive tomorrow or on Friday."*).
5. **Unscoped & Unqualified Local Relationships Implying Sentence Grammaticality ([server/learner_explanations.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py)):**
   - Positive local matches (`I` + `am` subject–verb agreement) were copied onto any ancestor covering either token (including root `S` and broken outer `VP` *"am scares tomorrow"*) without qualifying that local agreement does not establish predicate or full-sentence grammaticality.
6. **Category-Only Lesson Recommendations ([web/js/explanation-evidence.js](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/web/js/explanation-evidence.js), [server/learner_explanations.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py)):**
   - All `FINDING_LESSONS` rendered generic `"Related grammar lesson"`, `C-COPULA` pointed to subordinate clauses (`#lesson/br-08/worked-example`), temporal adjuncts pointed to `NP`/`PP`, and category-conflicted nodes still linked to misleading raw POS lessons.

## 2. Generalized Fixes & Cross-Layer Consistency Invariants

1. **Direct Temporal NP Adjuncts ([final_d1_projection.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/final_d1_projection.py#L589-L593), [L839-L840](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/final_d1_projection.py#L839-L840), [L899-L904](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/final_d1_projection.py#L899-L904), [L1383-L1390](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/final_d1_projection.py#L1383-L1390)):**
   - Bare temporal NPs project directly as `NP` adjuncts (`VP -> [VP, NP]`, matching Burton–Roberts `ch5-d3` & `ch6-69`). `temporal_pp()` is restricted strictly to mixed `NP CC PP` / `PP CC NP` coordination (*"tomorrow or on Friday"*).
2. **Copular Complement Compatibility & Auxiliary `be + VBZ` Propagation ([ruleset.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/ruleset.py#L5779-L5786), [L6791-L6859](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/ruleset.py#L6791-L6859); [findings.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/findings.py#L224-L234), [L404-L416](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/findings.py#L404-L416), [L483](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/findings.py#L483), [L545](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/findings.py#L545)):**
   - Added Rule `6.25` (`C-COPULA`) for strictly singular personal pronoun (`I`, `he`, `she`) + copular `be` (`am`, `is`, `was`) + bare `NNS` complement, with explicit exceptions protecting plural subjects (*"They are teachers."*, *"Those are scares."*), inanimate/abstract specificational subjects (*"The problem is delays."*), reciprocal predicates (*"I am friends with her."*), and singularia/idiomatic forms. Distinguishes category-conflicted verbal/nominal ambiguity (*"I am scares tomorrow."*) from pure number mismatch (*"I am teachers."*) and **withholds automatic rewrite** where intended meaning is ambiguous (*scared* vs. *scary* vs. *scaring* vs. *a teacher* vs. *We are teachers*).
   - Corroborated `be + VBZ` (`"She is sleeps today."`) in `local_relationships()` so `C-AUX-FORM` propagates into `learner_analysis` with target `sleeps`, related `is`, and validated revision `"She is sleeping today."`.
   - Updated `_evidence_token_ids()` to read both `supporting` and `conflict` evidence lists, surfacing non-erroneous related tokens (`I`, `am`) distinctly from error targets (`scares`, `teachers`).
   - Exempted coordinated temporal `NP + PP` adjuncts from `C-INTRANSITIVE-OBJECT`.
3. **Cross-Layer Invariants & Qualified Explanations ([learner_explanations.py](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py#L450-L600), [L676-L735](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py#L676-L735), [L833-L975](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py#L833-L975), [L1060-L1200](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/server/learner_explanations.py#L1060-L1200)):**
   - `_pos_tag_compatible()` + `_has_category_conflict()` detect spaCy UPOS vs. PTB/Benepar category contradictions (`ADJ` vs. `NNS`). Conflicted words/NPs/NOMs and their tentative empty `DET` slots are explicitly qualified instead of asserting unconfirmed POS/roles.
   - `_dep_role_phrase(..., node_label=...)` enforces category–function compatibility: `acomp` is never rendered as *"predicative adjective complement"* on `N`/`NP` (non-conflicted plural nominal predicates like *"Those are scares."* describe *"predicative complement of 'are'"*), `npadvmod` describes temporal adverbial modifiers accurately, and the *"It functions and serves as"* defect is eliminated.
   - `_describe_empty_node()` names the actual syntactic licensor for empty nodes: Burton–Roberts zero determiner (`DET [EMPTY]`) before bare plural/uncountable/temporal nouns (*"no spoken article is omitted"*), determiner/quantifier-licensed nominal ellipsis (`E` under `N` citing *"Those"*), auxiliary-licensed VP ellipsis (`E` under `VP`), movement gaps (`•`), and tentative conflicted placeholders.
   - Copular `VP` prose qualifies *"whether X is a licensed predicative complement here is not established"* whenever its complement is conflicted or flagged.
   - Positive local relationships attach strictly to participating tokens/minimal phrases (`covered.issubset(participants)`) and are qualified whenever `verdict != 'grammatical'` or `findings` is non-empty (*"Locally, ... This local match does not establish that the rest of the sentence is grammatical."*).
   - Emits `learner_analysis['consistency']` audit metadata (`category_conflicts`, `unlicensed_empty_nodes`, `local_only_verdict_qualified`) and concept-grounded per-node `lesson` / `suppress_category_lesson` fields.
4. **Concept-Grounded Lesson Routing ([explanation-evidence.js](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/web/js/explanation-evidence.js#L424-L450), [L526-L545](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/web/js/explanation-evidence.js#L526-L545)):**
   - Added `predicative` (`#lesson/br-04/predicatives`) and descriptive concept labels for all 23 `FINDING_LESSONS` routes, honored server-supplied `selected.lesson` (e.g., `#lesson/br-05/concepts` for temporal adjuncts, `#lesson/br-04/predicatives` for predicative complements), and suppressed misleading category fallbacks when `selected.suppress_category_lesson` is true.

## 4. Remaining Limitations & Evidence Boundaries

- **Human Evaluation Remains Unmeasured:** Automated consistency checks and browser tests confirm structural/evidential alignment against Burton–Roberts and CGEL conventions, **not** human pedagogical validation; all human evaluation worksheets remain blank and unfabricated.
- **Ambiguous Predicate Sub-Cases:** Non-pronominal animate singular subjects with bare plural predicates (e.g., *"The student is teachers."* vs. specificational *"The problem is delays."*) remain conservatively outside Rule `6.25` to prevent false positives on inanimate/abstract specificational clauses when semantic animacy is not deterministically guaranteed.
- **Mixed Temporal Coordination (`NP CC PP`):** In constructions like *"She will arrive tomorrow or on Friday."*, Burton–Roberts coordination symmetry still inserts an empty `P` placeholder on the NP conjunct; the explanation explicitly marks it as an alignment placeholder rather than an omitted spoken preposition and records it in `consistency['unlicensed_empty_nodes']`.
- **Legacy Browser Selectors:** Pre-existing tests [test_quiz_v2_browser.js](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/scripts/test_quiz_v2_browser.js) and [test_builder_generator_browser.mjs](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/scripts/test_builder_generator_browser.mjs) remain incompatible with [web/index.html](file:///Users/kiyel/Desktop/SynTutor/web/index.html) due to their obsolete `.sidebar-item[data-view="builder"]` selector.
