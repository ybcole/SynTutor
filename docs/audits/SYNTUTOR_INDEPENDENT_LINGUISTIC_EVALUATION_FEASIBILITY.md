# SynTutor independent linguistic evaluation feasibility

**Recommendation.** Keep the integrated technical study for the thesis claim that SynTutor carries syntax-tree evidence through to learner feedback. For linguistic accuracy, run separate, sealed comparisons on published annotations. Make unfamiliar FINAL-D1 tree accuracy a distinct question. The current textbook results show agreement with a familiar source under the specified scorer. Excluding exact lookup removes retrieval from that comparison, while prior exposure to the figures still limits the generalization claim. ## Obtain and seal cases without selecting on results

1. **Define the scope before sampling.** Use the actual enabled rules and documented reference categories to choose supported constructions. Use BLiMP pairs to isolate a contrast, document-linked learner writing to measure edits and evidence, and independently annotated natural sentences for structural comparisons. Predeclare construction, proficiency or genre strata and any supported-scope exclusions. Preserve input context and both members of a pair. Select by a seeded rule within eligible strata. Separately report an all-eligible reference sample and any supported-construction subset, retaining difficult cases and alignment failures in the cohort flow.
2. **Screen development exposure and training overlap separately.** Match exact and normalized source, target and revised text against fixtures, textbook lookup records, development corpora, tests, prompts, previous holdouts and predictions. Add near-duplicate screening and review short items and templates manually. Quarantine entire essays, repeated authors where IDs permit, and linked EXPECT variants. Within BLiMP, treat the paradigm as a reporting stratum and group near-shared templates and pairs. Lock the evaluator and record model IDs, hashes, training disclosures and unresolved exposure. Report “screened against known SynTutor development data” for a public benchmark. Public text may appear in opaque pretraining. Exclude CoLA from independent hybrid evidence because the advisory model was fine-tuned on it. Inspect the parser's disclosed WSJ data and the installed spaCy model's OntoNotes and vector sources before structural sampling. Similar annotation guidelines alone establish no sentence overlap. [Advisory model](https://huggingface.co/textattack/roberta-base-CoLA), [Benepar model details](https://github.com/nikitakit/self-attentive-parser#available-models)
3. **Make retrieval impossible in the projection comparison.** The current lookup matches lexical atoms under the relevant chapter/profile, so punctuation changes may still retrieve a textbook tree. Screen all fixture profiles, then run the existing `normalize_benepar_candidate(analyze(text), convention_profile())` path for projection scoring. In the separate live-API comparison, preserve the provenance trace and flag `EXACT_IMG_GOLD_REFERENCE` hits as retrieval evidence. The existing direct projection scorer already bypasses lookup. Keep retrieval parity, projected structural accuracy and technical availability as separate results. [Lookup implementation](../../server/burton_roberts.py#L435), [existing projection scorer](technical_evaluation/run_real_world_reliability.py#L70)
4. **Give the reference custodian control of the holdout.** A team member can check published labels, transcriptions and matches without seeing predictions. Freeze source revisions, IDs, normalization and crosswalks, reference scope, seed, case membership and exclusions before viewing outputs. Run once with a sealed manifest, preserve all failures, and replace a revealed set before claiming independence for another round of tuning. For a stronger pretraining-exposure claim, seek independently annotated texts with documented original creation dates after all frozen model checkpoints, or collect natural writing prospectively with permission and independent annotation. A recent corpus release can contain older text.

### Read-only provenance check

The local W&I v2.1 archive contains **350 development essays** with essay IDs, original text and character edits. Its converter treats null replacements as detection-only. Normalized substring matching within origin bands linked **115/120** Phase 2–3 W&I cases to one essay occurrence and returned five ambiguous matches. Comparing EXPECT source variants to original essays and EXPECT targets to essays reconstructed from the released edits linked **155/169** Phase 2–3 EXPECT annotations to one essay occurrence, with 14 still unmatched. Both W&I and both EXPECT learner inputs from the feasibility pilot also linked provisionally. The union of all candidate exposure matches covered **173 essays**. This suggests the published JSON can support document-level screening. [Release and corpus formats](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/nl/bea2019st/), [existing corpus provenance](SYNTUTOR_CODEX_PHASE3_BENCHMARK_RELIABILITY.md#reproduction-and-artifact-inventory)

After excluding those linked essays and known annotation IDs, **462 EXPECT annotation rows from 132 A/B/C essays** had a single candidate essay occurrence and both a source edit anchor and a separate witness. This is a reserve inventory, before manual alignment checks, supported-scope filtering and comparison with all available development material. It does not establish 462 independent units. Prior EXPECT annotations came from the native band. Keep unresolved native provenance out of the proposed A/B/C learner comparison and check cross-band duplicates. Before admitting a candidate, validate its original offsets or edit/context against the matched essay. Quarantine ambiguous links. For reproduction, replay non-null character edits from right to left for each annotator, compare NFKC casefolded word-character strings with spacing and punctuation removed, and remove EXPECT `[NONE]` slots before comparison. Use the known Phase 2 manifest and pilot IDs as the initial exposure lists. No candidate was scored or frozen here. [Pilot record IDs](SYNTUTOR_END_TO_END_EVALUATION_METHODOLOGY.md#published-input-records), [EXPECT source-variant construction](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.413/)

## Scoring, sample size and the gold-set decision

- **Use one denominator per claim.** For BLiMP, use correct verdicts on both members over all eligible pairs as the primary rules-only comparison. Count abstention as a missed pair and report decision coverage and sentence-level F1/MCC separately. Hybrid performance should state the advisory model and its decisive coverage. For W&I report ERRANT P/R/F0.5, revision coverage and processing failures. For EXPECT publish edit-anchor coverage and evidence P/R/F1 both over eligible reference evidence and conditional on matching the edit. Keep original adapter results as historical evidence. For structure, report aligned labeled span F1 with canonical availability, alignment losses and missing spans. Score features, movement/gaps and complete agreement only where the reference actually marks them. Preserve accepted alternative trees and source triangles without inventing their contents.
- **Choose precision and independent units first.** The 24-case pilot tests feasibility. For a simple independent proportion, the conservative planning approximation `1.96²/(4w²)` gives roughly **97 independent units** for a 95% interval with ±10 percentage-point precision, or **385** for ±5 points. These are illustrations for choosing a precision target. Use pairs, documents or author groups as sampling units where observations are dependent. For F-scores and version comparisons, plan from pilot error and coverage rates and use cluster-aware or paired uncertainty estimates. Inflate recruitment for missing references and alignments. A small convenience subset supports descriptive claims about that set. [NIST Technical Note 2119](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/TechnicalNotes/NIST.TN.2119.pdf)
- **A small reference-backed diagnostic set is feasible. Complete novel gold is currently blocked.** Engineers can verify exact published labels, edits, witness indices and visibly marked diagrams through independent source checks. They can also measure technical fidelity to the API, including uncertainty and evidence routing. The existing pilot has **0/24** complete references for the same sentence. Joining datasets helps only for exact shared inputs and compatible annotations. Full novel FINAL-D1 trees, ambiguity and intended meaning, alternative repairs and the truth of novel explanations require independently authored compatible references or qualified human adjudication. Agreement among SynTutor, another parser or a language model cannot supply that reference. Educational effectiveness requires measured learner outcomes.
