# SynTutor evaluation readiness

**Recommendation.** Use the integrated technical conformance study as the primary evaluation for the current thesis scope. It tests whether the syntax tree and grammatical evidence reach the correct learner-facing views and retain their scope and certainty. Report independent benchmark comparisons separately. [Methodology and proposed acceptance gate](SYNTUTOR_END_TO_END_EVALUATION_METHODOLOGY.md#6-primary-technical-evaluation-that-the-team-can-execute)

| Evaluation | Readiness | Defensible result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generator and Builder workflow conformance | **Feasible with the current team.** Existing browser workflows provide the entry points. A study-only collector and six scorer controls are needed before the planned run. | Passes over 18 fixed scenarios, delivery over four required pathways, and correct classifications over six evaluator controls. Verify source and selection identity, evidence routing, correction replay, qualified fallback and recovery. |
| 24-case feasibility pilot | **Feasible with bounded references.** Exact records were verified for 12 inputs. The other 12 are authored stress probes. | Compare eight BLiMP labels in four pairs, two marked M2 edits, and two EXPECT types/edits with six source-side witnesses. Use the authored cases for technical checks and disclose any prespecified source-derived spot checks separately. |
| Existing linguistic benchmarks | **Available as supporting evidence.** Original scoring and Phase 3 results are preserved. | Published acceptability labels, exact annotated edit agreement, evidence-word overlap and agreement with B–R figure spans under their stated denominators. Report the projection and source-lookup paths separately. |
| Joint linguistic accuracy on the pilot | **Blocked.** Zero of 24 cases has complete independent references. None matches an exact tree in the archived local B–R source inventory, and none has a complete explanation-proposition reference. | Complete-analysis accuracy is unavailable. Multiple valid trees, intended readings, meaning-preserving alternatives and full explanations require exact-source references or qualified independent review. |
| Independent final linguistic validation | **Blocked.** It needs new sealed cases, applicable complete references and documented independence. The learner-record IDs checked here do not establish document-level separation. | The 24-case pilot measures feasibility. Its known inputs provide no unseen-validation result. |
| Educational effectiveness | **Outside this technical study.** It requires learner outcome data. | Technical conformance and explanation traceability can substantiate the framework's operation. A transfer or retention claim needs a separate learner study. |

The audit checked original corpus fields and locators for all 12 published inputs and compared all 24 inputs with 242 sentence-bearing B–R records. It added source locators and claim limits for the 12 authored probes. The pilot, evaluator controls and primary workflow study remain unrun under this protocol.
