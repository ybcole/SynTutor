# SynTutor manuscript–system alignment audit

**Verdict: broadly aligned in design and supported functionality, but not fully aligned as a description of the current system’s measured performance.** The manuscript is strongest where it distinguishes textbook agreement, regression checks, grammar-feedback outcomes, and user-study results. It should identify the evaluated builds explicitly and narrow several claims before submission. There is no basis for claiming unrestricted parsing accuracy or independently validated explanation correctness.

**Scope (24 November 2026):** the supplied 154-page *Copy of SynTutor: An Explainable AI-Based Framework for Syntax Tree-Driven Grammar Analysis and Learning Using NLP* PDF (SHA-256 `93e7e23bf0e5ec90cc6ac5f094fa92ae7a57029f0788922e5d6a2a1707821bea`); local branch `fix/syntutor-system-reliability-xai`, HEAD `05dfdf0f9d731d8ad783b5d6b50e55cbbb08f30f` **plus** the uncommitted 26-sentence backend remediation and frontend changes. Page numbers below are those printed in the PDF. This is not an audit of the hosted deployment, third-party service provisioning, every literature citation, or the original participant records.

## 2. Technical findings and manuscript corrections

### H01 — Structural agreement, not confirmatory parser accuracy

- **Reproduced on the current checkout:** 213/216 normalized (98.61%); 204/216 yield-aligned (94.44%); **83.35% triangle-aware F1** and **67.00% unmasked labeled F1** on the 204 aligned figures; **62.98% unmasked labeled F1 across all 216 attempts**. Add the all-attempt figure to Table 5.2 so the effect of alignment and triangle masking is visible. These are constituent measures, not the proportion of fully correct trees.
- **The 91.56% result reproduces, but its subset is mislabeled.** Selecting Chapter 3–10 reference diagrams with **no multiword triangle** gives 66 eligible figures, of which 65 aligned and were scored (one yield mismatch: `ch7-app-17`). Eleven of the 65 scored references still have single-token triangles. A literal “no triangles” filter gives **90.37% F1 on 54 aligned figures**. In the abstract, §§3.3.8, 4.5.5 and 5.1.2, Table 5.2, the summary and conclusions, say “91.56% labeled F1 on 65 yield-aligned Chapter 3–10 diagrams without multiword triangles (66 eligible)” rather than “65 complete, non-triangle diagrams.”
- The current V3 rerun also reproduced **17/17** cases (62/62 published relations) and **50/50** each for compliance and conversion. These are **regressions on known cases**, not complete-tree gold or an inferential accuracy test across complexity levels. The **45/48** development/adversarial result (previously 13/48) was corroborated by the historical report, not rerun in this audit.
- **H01 remains undetermined as worded.** The textbook references were available during development, lookup was bypassed for scoring, and no unseen, independently adjudicated complete B–R reference set was available. The metric is for the integrated normalization pipeline, not isolated Benepar accuracy. Keep the caveats already present on pp. 102–104 and 127.

### H02 — A paired benchmark comparison, not an expert rating of XAI

**The local reruns did not reproduce every historical number.** The same V3 inputs and scorer produced the following on this machine (counts out of 100):

| Measure | PDF’s historical Arm A | Local Arm A (clean HEAD and working tree) | Local Arm B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Clean feedback (O4) | 89 | 89 | 65 |
| Decided and clean (O4c) | 89 | **88** | 65 |
| Correct verdict (O1) | 93 | **92** | 67 |
| Target overlap (O2) | 91 | 91 | 53 |
| Accepted correction outcome (O3) | 91 | 91 | 51 |
| Joint outcome adequacy | 88 | **87** | 50 |
| Abstentions | 0 | **1** | 0 |

The abstention (`V3-G36`, *Maria simply gazed at the bollard she had just demolished.*) had a normalized tree, no flagged error and no rewrite. It counted as clean under O4 but not as a correct judgment, a decided-clean result or a jointly adequate outcome. Both local runs still favored Arm A on O4 (Holm `p = 0.000232334`) and decided-clean O4c (Holm `p = 0.000294077`, recomputed from unrounded exact tests across six binary outcomes). On the grammatical half, Arm A accepted **43/50**, flagged six and abstained once; on the ungrammatical half, it detected **49/50**. Do not use the historical binary confusion matrix to describe these reruns. D1 and D2 remained 93/100 and 100/100 under their automated definitions.

The abstention also occurred on **clean HEAD**, so it is **not attributable to the uncommitted changes**. Comparing local per-item outputs, the working tree preserved all 100 verdicts and the 100 correction fields scored by V3 (learner revision if present, API correction otherwise). It nevertheless populated the separate learner revision field on five items and narrowed the error target/rule packet on `V3-U38`. Unchanged scores do not imply identical learner-facing output. The cause of the discrepancy with the original run was not established; these are same-dataset reruns, not independent replications.

- The historical V3 report supports Table 5.1a’s main **89/100 versus 65/100 clean-feedback** result and the other reported outcome counts. Table 5.1d’s `TP=49, FP=6, FN=1, TN=44` gives **93.00% accuracy, 89.09% precision, 98.00% recall, 93.33% F1, and 88.00% specificity**, as stated. Keep the 100-item measures distinct from **47/50** localization and **45/50** correction on the ungrammatical half.
- **Correct the statistical precision.** From the [historical report’s discordant counts](h01_h02_evaluation_methodology/H01_H02_COMPREHENSIVE_RELIABILITY_IMPROVEMENT_REPORT.md#f2-h02--rule-grounded-arm-a-vs-ungrounded-neural-baseline-arm-b), exact two-sided McNemar for O4 (31:7) gives `p = 0.000116167`; Holm across the six reported binary outcomes gives `p = 0.000232334`, consistent with the PDF. For joint adequacy (44:6), exact `p = 3.24374 × 10⁻⁸` becomes **Holm `p = 1.62187 × 10⁻⁷`**, not `< 10⁻⁷` (pp. 100, 106). O1’s adjusted value is `0.0000730277`, not `0.000072` when inputs are not rounded before adjustment (p. 99). O2 and O3’s stated bounds still hold. Disclose the six-test family, including the decided-clean row that equaled O4 in the historical run, and apply Holm before rounding. This does not change the historical H02 decision.
- **Use scorer-accurate terms.** O2 checks position-aware **overlap** with an annotated target after rejecting overly broad spans; it does not require identical boundaries or judge every displayed marker. O3 matches a normalized correction to the accepted reference list; a non-match is not automatically linguistically invalid. O4 means no demonstrable issue **under the rubric** and can credit abstention or missing feedback; report decided-clean O4 and abstentions separately when they differ. D2 checks node IDs and source-channel consistency **where those fields are present**; `100/100` means no violations flagged by those checks, not that every explanation had a source or was correct. See the [V3 scorer](technical_evaluation/run_textbook_h01_h02_v3.py).
- **Limit inference to this sample and comparator.** Arm B uses a CoLA classifier and perturbation-based localization/correction; it does not provide a like-for-like node explanation. The comparison does not isolate the causal effect of rule grounding from all other differences. On p. 95, prefer “frozen, repository-overlap-checked textbook benchmark” to unqualified “independent”; the report documents overlap checks, not independent linguistic adjudication or absence from pretrained data. The historical preservation audit checked **verdicts and corrections only**; revise “whose outcomes were preserved” to name those fields, not O2, O4, grounding, or explanation prose. The historical reports cite transient output directories; the archived historical per-item predictions and preservation manifest were not available in the reviewed checkout. Archive those with the exact evaluated commit, model revisions, data/scorer hashes and per-item results.

## 3. Student outcomes and usability

| Claim | Audit finding |
|---|---|
| **H03 / student gains** (pp. 70–73, 114–117, 128; Appendix A.5) | **Total-score results reproduced** from the 25 published pairs per group: experimental `21.36 → 24.72` (gain `3.36`, paired `t(24)=4.7068`, `p=0.0000875`); control `19.12 → 19.40` (gain `0.28`, `t(24)=0.3600`, `p=0.7220`); between-group gain `3.08`, Welch `t(47.65)=2.9173`, `p=0.00537`, 95% CI `[0.9568, 5.2032]`. The group sizes, not the 400 pages, are the units in these calculations. Class membership largely determined condition, baseline scores differed, and the researcher-scored forms have unresolved item ambiguities and unverified equivalence. Say **“larger observed short-term gains,”** not “SynTutor caused improvement.” The PDF’s written Ha3 (p. 13) compares with controls **without SynTutor**; pp. 70, 116 and 128 refer to an “original” hypothesis against commercial/traditional grammar checkers. Remove that inconsistency or cite a dated earlier protocol explicitly. Actual control tool use was not recorded. Section-level gains and rubric scores were not independently verified from the appendix’s total-score pairs. |
| **H04 / perceived usability and quality** (pp. 119–126, 128) | **Descriptively favorable, not a hypothesis rejection or certification.** The student mean `71.10` and published SD `18.94` at `n=25` imply an approximate t interval of `[63.28, 78.92]`, including 68. The two published professional scores `60.00` and `82.50` give mean `71.25` and sample SD `15.91`. Report “both observed means exceeded the 68-point reference average”; remove “descriptive rejection of H04” on p. 128 and repair the sentence fragment on p. 120. The ISO-based scores measure perceptions, and raw item-level survey responses were not supplied for this audit. Neither instrument establishes accessibility, engineering reliability, or explanation correctness. |

The section-level pre- and post-test Welch contrasts are consistent with the published rounded group means and SDs in Tables 5.6–5.7. That check does not reconstruct the individual section gains or verify the underlying scores.

The [repository’s Chapter I draft](../research/CHAPTER1_REVISED.md) contains the traditional-checker Ha3 wording and a different H02 formulation about the Matrix and licensing layers. That does not establish which version was approved before evaluation, but it does show that the PDF and repository drafts should not be called verbatim matches. If the PDF’s hypotheses are revised operational versions, identify them as such rather than saying in §3.7 that all four pairs were retained unchanged.

## 4. Submission priorities

1. Pin the evaluated build(s) in the abstract, methods, results and conclusions. Label original V3 comparisons, post-structural-fix regressions, and newer challenge checks separately. Do not present a known-set rerun as an independent replication. `origin/main` (`5f582552` locally) includes a separate Chapter 5 development-corpus script; it is not the V3 source or a substitute for the frozen results.
2. Fix the Holm results and definitions of localization, correction, clean feedback, and provenance; add the all-attempt H01 figure and correct the Canvas/SVG description.
3. Resolve the Ha3 comparator wording and H04 decision language. Keep nonrandom allocation, baseline imbalance, lack of rater agreement and the brief intervention visible when interpreting outcomes.
4. Regenerate the contents and lists of figures/tables (e.g. Chapter IV is on p. 75, not 74, and Chapter V on p. 96, not 95); include Appendix E in the contents. Use the same build and scorer labels on any refreshed result figures.
5. For stronger future claims, freeze an unseen, independently adjudicated complete-tree set and obtain blinded linguist ratings of explanations and revisions, including unresolved cases. Report coverage with accuracy rather than treating abstention as error-free performance.
