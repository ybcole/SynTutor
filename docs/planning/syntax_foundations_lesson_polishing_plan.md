# Syntax Foundations polishing plan for lessons 3–12

Use the [rollout plan](syntax_foundations_lesson_rollout.md), [M4 launch curriculum](../curriculum/README.md), reviewed course catalog, and Chapters 1–2 as the implementation references. Chapter 1 shows how to make a contrast visible. Chapter 2 places a small learner decision and feedback before construction, then transfers the relationship to a fresh example. Keep the six-page sequence, the reviewed Burton-Roberts notation and scope, and the Cambridge companion’s separate track.

## Implementation priorities

1. Synchronise selection, caption, word highlighting, and pressed controls on worked trees. Describe a gap by its position in the tree, even when it covers no words. Give Chapter 9 separate controls for its two gaps. Use local teaching captions so the activity works with the lesson’s authored tree.
2. Add one unscored transfer activity per lesson. Use direct word or boundary selection, a marked structural contrast, or a short text construction. Ask learners to try the new example before they reveal the analysis. Keep teaching contrasts and examples visible. Use clear feedback and allow retries.
3. Give embedded branch exercises visible recovery controls. Undo the last edit or restore the starting exercise from the same canvas. Make a restart undoable and preserve the existing exercise ID and draft. When a branch is detached or attached to the wrong node, identify the relationship to repair and point to the intended parent.
