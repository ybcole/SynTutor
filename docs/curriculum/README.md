# M4 launch curriculum

The launch course has 11 B-R core lessons and one Cambridge companion. These are
focused introductory units across the chapter spine, not an exhaustive
reproduction of either textbook. Each unit has one assessed outcome, four
original formative questions and a six-stage instructional flow. Core Chapters
1–10 have original canonical worked trees; Chapter 11 is a text-only synthesis.

Each row maps its outcome `o1` to guided practice, an original transfer task and
an original exit check. Difficulty/effort, prerequisite version, concept ID,
source IDs and exact expected answers live in `web/content/lessons/course.json`.
Its local concept registry makes the IDs explicit; `CLS-001` is linked as a
classification record, never as an enabled runtime constraint. B-R 2 and 8
include the selected Cambridge terminology insight. Other core lessons do not
force unrelated Cambridge material into the flow.

The Cambridge track has 17 further topic placeholders: auxiliaries, valency,
NPs, adjective/adverb categories, prepositions, adjuncts, negation, clause types,
subordination, relatives, comparatives, non-finites, coordination, information
packaging, morphology, word formation and punctuation. Their underlying
extractions are inventoried, but their lessons/answers are **not published** or
counted as completed coverage. The manifest does not certify their content.

Reading any published lesson is available without an XP/account gate. The
prerequisite sequence controls completion credit, not access to explanations.
The guided tree activity fills a highlighted label/feature on the authored
model. It does not claim to deliver M6's free-form chapter-aware editor.
