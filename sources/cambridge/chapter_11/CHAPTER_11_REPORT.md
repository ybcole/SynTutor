## Interpretation safeguards
- Subordinate is not identical to syntactic dependent: some subordinate-form clauses appear as elliptical responses, rare exclamatives or supplements.
- Main versus subordinate clause status cannot be inferred simply from sentence containment; a clause may be the head of the containing construction.
- No overt marker such as `that` is required in every structurally subordinate content clause. The conditions for `that` omission are deferred to §3.1.
- Some dependent clauses can show **main-clause interrogative** inversion, but the distribution is limited and must be verified against §5.3.1 before generalisation.
- The finite classification includes subjunctive clauses even with a plain-form verb.
- No new grammar-error constraints were inferred from descriptive classifications.
