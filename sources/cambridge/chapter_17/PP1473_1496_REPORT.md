## Explicit high-risk safeguards and future QA

- **1473–1475:** Bound-variable readings (including singular they), plural distributions and donkey anaphora are not equivalent to strict coreference. The rare changing-possessor use of it has unresolved licensing conditions, so it is descriptive, not a detector.
- **1475–1480:** First-mention cataphora differs sharply from repeat mention; syntactic embedding, fronting, pragmatic coordination, discourse distance and style affect acceptability. Rhetorical delayed identification and concessive/conditional coordination are legitimate English.
- **1480–1483:** An independent later main clause, right dislocation, dummy it in extraposition/clefts, weather/time/place and idiomatic it are different constructions; none licences the others by broad substitution.
