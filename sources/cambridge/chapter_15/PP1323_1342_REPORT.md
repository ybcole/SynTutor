## High-risk interpretation guards

- p. 1324: Coordination normally requires common syntactic and semantic function, but unlike categories can be coordinated; special joint and rhetorical forms are exceptions.
- pp. 1324–1325: The infinitival/gerund-participial pairing is a specific restriction, not an across-the-board ban on unlike categories.
- pp. 1331–1332: Single versus multiple genitive constructions have joint/distributive and pronoun-case subtleties; no universal prescriptive apostrophe rule is inferred.
- pp. 1333–1335: Interrogative scope and nominal number must be distinguished from semantic anomaly and style.
- pp. 1337–1342: Gapping and right nonce-constituent coordination are valid non-basic structures; negative scope can prevent naive expansion to repeated full clauses.
- pp. 1335–1336: Unlike-level examples are acknowledged as marginal/uncertain, not automatically erroneous.
