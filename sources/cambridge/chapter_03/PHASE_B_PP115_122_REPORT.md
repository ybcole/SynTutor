## Rule totals and exceptions

Added **31 records** (1 constraint, 24 classification, 1 stylistic_or_prescriptive, 5 descriptive_generalization). The only new diagnostic specification concerns invalid bare stranding of the aspectual former-habitual *used*; it has two original minimal pairs and four false-positive guards. It is *not* yet activated as a runtime detector.

- The source distinguishes auxiliary-like and lexical *used to* across registers, spelling conventions, and speaker generations. Informal and dialectal negatives must not be globally prohibited.
- Tense morphology, aspectual viewpoint, and modal interpretation are separate; a preterite is not always a past-time reference, and *will* is treated as a modal auxiliary.
- Stative predicates generally resist progressive aspect, but temporary behavioural readings are possible. Habitual or scheduled simple-present occurrences are not automatically errors.
- A verb's activity, accomplishment or achievement reading often depends on its arguments. Bounded object and duration adjunct combinations therefore need context.
- Reaching the endpoint is entailed by *finish* with an accomplishment, not by *stop*. Progressive accomplishments can be incomplete; strict and extendable achievements differ.
